About Us

Background Story

Mthwana Logistics was founded by Ntokozo Ntsika Mthwana to meet growing demand for safe, professional fuel delivery across the Southern African region. We began with a small but modern fleet and a clear focus on safety and client service.

Mission

To deliver petroleum safely, efficiently, and with the highest standards of professionalism.

Vision

To be the trusted logistics partner for fuel suppliers and industrial clients across Southern Africa.

Core Values

  • Safety
  • Integrity
  • Reliability
  • Client Commitment
  • Continuous Improvement
